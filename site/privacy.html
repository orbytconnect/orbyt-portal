Orbyt Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Orbyt LLC ("Orbyt," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares personal information through our website at orbytconnect.com, our portal at portal.orbytconnect.com, and our emails (together, the "Services"). Orbyt runs a vendor network that introduces real estate agents to local vendors who help get homes ready to list, sell, or move into.

The Services are for real estate professionals and businesses. If you use the Services, the Orbyt Agent Terms of Use or Vendor Terms of Use also apply to you.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us.

  • Account details: your name, email address, mobile phone number, password, and the areas you work in.
  • For agents: your brokerage, and details about the properties and projects you send us, such as the address, target dates, the services the home needs, notes, and your client's name or contact details if you choose to share them.
  • For vendors: your company name, website, services, years in business, team size, lead time, typical pricing, licenses, insurance information and certificates, photos of your work, and the agent references you list. We may contact those references.
  • Work through the portal: bids, quotes and files you upload, schedules, messages, job amounts, invoices, and reviews.
  • Anything else you send us, such as form submissions on our website and emails to us.

Information collected automatically.

  • Terms records: when you agree to our Terms of Use, we record the version, the date and time, your IP address, and your browser type, so we have a record of the agreement.
  • Technical information: our hosting and email providers keep standard logs, such as IP addresses, browser type, pages requested, and whether an email was delivered.
  • Browser storage: the portal stores your sign-in session and a few display settings in your browser so you stay signed in. The portal does not use advertising or analytics trackers. Our website is hosted by Wix, which may use cookies to run the site and measure visits.

Information from others. An agent may share details about a property or a client with us so we can find the right vendors. Agents and vendors may write reviews about each other. Your references may tell us about your work.

2. How we use information

  • To run the network: review sign-ups, check references, licenses and insurance, match agents with vendors, and make introductions.
  • To run the portal: create and secure accounts, show projects, bids and quotes, and let agents share a private quotes page with their clients.
  • To send emails about your account and your projects, such as new bid requests, bid updates, approvals, and password resets.
  • To calculate, invoice, and collect vendor fees, and to confirm that fees were reported correctly.
  • To keep records of agreements, prevent fraud and misuse, enforce our terms, and meet legal obligations.
  • To improve the Services, including by looking at information that does not identify anyone.
  • To tell you about Orbyt. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time. If you give us a mobile number and agree to texts, we may text you about matches and your account. Reply STOP to opt out.

3. How we share information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

  • Between agents and vendors. Sharing is how the network works. When we introduce a vendor, we share the vendor's profile, contact details, pricing and reviews with the agent, and the property and project details the vendor needs, including the agent's contact details, with the vendor. Vendor reviews are shown to other agents.
  • With people an agent chooses. Agents can add teammates to a project and can share a private link that shows their client vendor names, prices, timelines and ratings.
  • With our service providers, who handle information for us under contracts that limit how they use it: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Netlify (portal hosting), Resend (email delivery), Wix (website hosting), and Google (email and documents). If we add online payments, a payment processor will handle card and bank details, and Orbyt will not store full card or bank account numbers.
  • For legal reasons, such as to comply with law or a valid legal request, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights and safety of Orbyt, our users, or others.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger, sale, or financing, subject to this policy.

4. How long we keep information

We keep account and project information while your account is active. After you close your account, we delete or de-identify it within a reasonable time, except what we need to keep for legal, tax, accounting, or dispute purposes, such as invoices, fee records, and records of your agreement to our terms. Reviews you wrote may stay up without your contact details.

5. Your choices

  • You can see and update most of your information in the portal.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your personal information, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing ashleigh@orbytconnect.com. We may need to confirm your identity, and we may keep information the law allows or requires us to keep.
  • You can turn off marketing emails using the unsubscribe link. We will still send emails about your account and projects.
  • California residents may ask whether we share personal information with third parties for their own direct marketing. We do not.
  • Some browsers send "Do Not Track" signals. The portal has no advertising or cross-site tracking, so there is nothing for those signals to turn off.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information, including encrypted connections, access controls that limit each account to its own projects, and private file storage. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private. Vendors and agents are responsible for protecting the client and property information they receive, including access codes.

7. Children

The Services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Where information is stored

Orbyt is based in California. Our portal database is hosted in the United States, and our service providers mainly store information there.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new "Last updated" date, and if a change is significant, we will tell you in the portal or by email.

10. Contact us

Questions or requests about your information can be sent to ashleigh@orbytconnect.com or to Orbyt LLC, 711 S Nardo Ave, Suite 308, Solana Beach, California 92075.
