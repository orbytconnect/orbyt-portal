# ORBYT CLOSING PARTNER MARKETING SERVICES AGREEMENT

*Last updated: November 10, 2026*

This Closing Partner Marketing Services Agreement (the **\"Agreement\"**) is between Orbyt LLC, a California limited liability company located at 711 S Nardo Ave, Suite 308, Solana Beach, California 92075 (**\"Orbyt\"**), and the mortgage lender, loan originator, title company, escrow company, insurance agency, or other settlement service provider that lists with Orbyt (**\"Partner\"**). Orbyt and Partner are each a \"party\" and together the \"parties.\"

**How Partner accepts this Agreement.** Partner accepts this Agreement, and it takes effect, when Partner checks the box or clicks to agree to it through the Orbyt website or portal (together, the **\"Portal\"**). No signature is required. The person accepting confirms they are authorized to bind Partner.

**Key terms.** Orbyt sells Partner marketing and advertising services. Orbyt does not refer, recommend, or steer anyone to Partner, and nothing Partner pays is for referrals. Section 3 sets out the fee: free for the first 90 days, then $20 a month.

## 1. Marketing Services

For the fee in Section 3, Orbyt provides Partner the following marketing services (the **\"Services\"**), on the same terms for every closing partner on the same plan:

**1.1 Directory listing.** A listing in the Orbyt closing partner directory, which real estate agents who use the Portal (**\"Agents\"**) can browse. The listing shows Partner's name, logo, services, service areas, license number, contact details, website, business overview document, and approved reviews.

**1.2 Spotlights.** Inclusion in Orbyt's closing partner spotlights in its emails and social media posts to Agents, rotated among listed partners.

**1.3 Events and education.** Invitations to Orbyt networking and educational events when Orbyt holds them, and the opportunity to co-host or present at Orbyt educational sessions, subject to availability.

Orbyt may improve or add to the Services. Orbyt will tell Partner in advance of any change that reduces them.

## 2. An Open Directory, Not Referrals

**2.1 Open to every licensed provider.** Any licensed provider of the services listed in the directory may list, subject to the same license and conduct requirements for everyone. Orbyt does not limit the number of partners in any category or area, and no partner is preferred, exclusive, or "approved" as a recommendation.

**2.2 No referrals or endorsements.** Orbyt does not refer, recommend, endorse, or steer any Agent or consumer to Partner. Agents see listed partners in a rotating order with a note that partners pay for advertising, that Orbyt does not recommend one over another, and that consumers are free to choose any provider. Orbyt does not introduce Agents to Partner on request.

**2.3 Nothing of value for referrals.** Orbyt does not pay, and Partner will not give, offer, or pay, anything of value to Orbyt, any Agent, or any consumer in exchange for, or tied to, a referral of settlement service business. The fee in Section 3 pays only for the Services and does not depend on the number of contacts, applications, loans, policies, or closings. Partner will not ask Orbyt or any Agent to steer business to Partner.

**2.4 The law comes first.** Both parties intend this Agreement to comply with the Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA) and its Regulation X, and with California law on title, escrow, mortgage, and insurance marketing and referrals. If any part of this Agreement or the Portal could be read to require a payment or benefit those laws prohibit, that part does not apply.

## 3. Fees

**3.1 First 90 days free.** The Services are free for ninety (90) days from the date Partner first accepts this Agreement.

**3.2 Monthly fee.** After that, the fee is twenty dollars ($20) per month, billed monthly in advance by card or invoice. The fee is a flat amount for the Services, the same for every partner on the same plan, and Orbyt has set it at the fair market value of comparable advertising and event services.

**3.3 Cancel anytime.** Partner may cancel at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the month already paid for, and the listing is then removed. Orbyt does not refund partial months.

**3.4 Late payment.** If a payment is not made within fifteen (15) days of its due date, Orbyt may pause Partner's listing until it is paid.

**3.5 Changes to the fee.** Orbyt may change the fee with at least thirty (30) days' written notice. Partner may cancel instead.

## 4. Partner's Responsibilities

**4.1 Licenses and compliance.** Partner holds, and will keep current, every license and registration its services require, including, as applicable, an NMLS identifier, a California Department of Real Estate or Department of Financial Protection and Innovation license, or a California Department of Insurance license. Partner is solely responsible for complying with all laws that apply to its services, including RESPA, the Truth in Lending Act, fair lending and fair housing laws, privacy and data security laws, and any required disclosures. Partner will tell Orbyt promptly if a license lapses or is suspended.

**4.2 Accurate listing and lawful advertising.** Partner will keep its listing, license information, and contact details accurate and up to date, and is responsible for making sure its listing and marketing materials comply with the advertising rules for its industry, including any required license numbers and disclosures.

**4.3 Contacting people.** Partner will contact an Agent's client only with that Agent's or that client's permission, and will follow all laws on calls, texts, and email, including the Telephone Consumer Protection Act.


## 5. Partner Is Independent. Orbyt Is Not a Party to Any Transaction

Partner is an independent business. Orbyt does not originate loans, issue title or insurance policies, hold escrow funds, give financial, legal, or tax advice, or take part in any transaction between Partner and an Agent or client. Partner is solely responsible for its services, advice, disclosures, pricing, and conduct.

## 6. Reviews and Content

Agents may review Partner, and approved reviews may appear on Partner's listing. Partner gives Orbyt a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to display the name, logo, profile, documents, and other content Partner adds to the Portal, to run and promote the network. Partner confirms it has the rights to that content and that it is accurate.

## 7. Confidentiality and Privacy

Each party will protect the other's non-public information and the personal information of Agents and clients it receives, and will use it only for the transaction it relates to. Orbyt's [Privacy Policy](https://portal.orbytconnect.com/privacy.html) explains how Orbyt handles personal information.

## 10. Ending

Either party may end this Agreement at any time with written notice, including by email, as described in Section 3.3. ## 11. Disputes

This Agreement are governed by California law. Before starting any proceeding, the party with a dispute will send the other a written notice and the parties will try in good faith to resolve it for thirty (30) days. Neither party will bring a claim as part of a class or representative action. Either party may bring a qualifying claim in small claims court instead.
