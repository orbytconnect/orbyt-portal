# ORBYT CLOSING PARTNER TERMS

*Last updated: November 3, 2026*

These Closing Partner Terms (the **\"Terms\"**) are a binding agreement between Orbyt LLC, a California limited liability company located at 711 S Nardo Ave, Suite 308, Solana Beach, California 92075 (**\"Orbyt\"**), and the mortgage lender, loan originator, title company, escrow company, insurance agency, or other settlement service provider that joins the Orbyt network as a closing partner (**\"Partner\"**). Orbyt and Partner are each a \"party\" and together the \"parties.\"

**How Partner accepts these Terms.** Partner accepts these Terms, and they take effect, on the earliest of the date Partner (a) checks the box or clicks to agree to these Terms when joining through the Orbyt website or portal (together, the **\"Portal\"**), or (b) accepts or responds to an Introduction. No signature is required. The person accepting confirms they are authorized to bind Partner.

**Key terms.** Section 2 explains that no one pays or receives anything for referrals. Section 3 explains that founding membership is free, and how any future membership fee would work.

## 1. How the Network Works for Partners

**1.1 Introductions.** Real estate agents (**\"Agents\"**) use the Portal to ask for help with their clients' transactions. When an Agent asks for a closing partner, Orbyt may introduce Partner to the Agent (an **\"Introduction\"**). Orbyt shares the Agent's contact details and the property and timing details Partner needs. Orbyt does not promise any number of Introductions.

**1.2 Choice stays with the Agent and the client.** Agents and their clients are always free to choose any lender, title company, escrow company, or insurance provider, inside or outside the network. Nothing in the Portal requires anyone to use Partner, and Partner will not tell any client otherwise.

**1.3 Orbyt's checks.** Orbyt may confirm Partner's licenses and references before approving Partner. These checks are limited. They are not an endorsement, guarantee, or legal review of Partner's services.

## 2. No Payments for Referrals

**2.1 Orbyt receives nothing for Introductions.** Orbyt does not charge Partner, and will not accept anything of value from Partner, for any Introduction, referral, or closed transaction. No fee, discount, gift, credit, or other benefit to Orbyt depends on the number of Introductions, referrals, applications, loans, policies, or closings.

**2.2 Partner gives nothing for referrals.** Partner will not give, offer, or pay anything of value to Orbyt, any Agent, or any client in exchange for, or tied to, an Introduction or a referral, and will not ask Orbyt or any Agent to steer business to Partner. This includes cash, gifts, discounts tied to referrals, paid marketing for referrals, and shared fees.

**2.3 The law comes first.** Both parties intend these Terms to comply with the Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA) and its Regulation X, and with California law on title, escrow, mortgage, and insurance referrals. If any part of these Terms or the Portal could be read to require a payment or benefit those laws prohibit, that part does not apply.

## 3. Founding Membership and Future Fees

**3.1 Free for founding partners.** Partners who join during Orbyt's founding period pay nothing to use the Portal.

**3.2 Any future fee.** Orbyt may later offer a paid membership for closing partners. If it does: (a) the fee will be a flat amount for access to the Portal and its tools, the same for every closing partner on the same plan, and set at a fair market value for those services; (b) the fee will never depend on the number of Introductions, referrals, loans, policies, or closings; (c) Orbyt will give Partner at least thirty (30) days' written notice, and Partner will only owe the fee if Partner chooses to continue; and (d) Partner can leave instead, with nothing owed for past Introductions.

## 4. Partner's Responsibilities

**4.1 Licenses and compliance.** Partner holds, and will keep current, every license and registration its services require, including, as applicable, an NMLS identifier, a California Department of Real Estate or Department of Financial Protection and Innovation license, or a California Department of Insurance license. Partner is solely responsible for complying with all laws that apply to its services, including RESPA, the Truth in Lending Act, fair lending and fair housing laws, privacy and data security laws, and any required disclosures. Partner will tell Orbyt promptly if a license lapses or is suspended.

**4.2 Accurate profile.** Partner will keep its profile, license information, and contact details accurate and up to date.

**4.3 Contacting clients.** Orbyt shares the Agent's contact details with Partner. Partner will contact an Agent's client only with that Agent's or that client's permission, and will follow all laws on calls, texts, and email, including the Telephone Consumer Protection Act.

**4.4 Responding to Introductions.** Partner will respond to each Introduction promptly and professionally, or tell Orbyt if it cannot help.

## 5. Partner Is Independent. Orbyt Is Not a Party to Any Transaction

Partner is an independent business. Orbyt does not originate loans, issue title or insurance policies, hold escrow funds, give financial, legal, or tax advice, or take part in any transaction between Partner and an Agent or client. Partner is solely responsible for its services, advice, disclosures, pricing, and conduct.

## 6. Reviews and Content

Agents may review Partner, and approved reviews may appear on Partner's profile. Partner gives Orbyt a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to display the name, logo, profile, documents, and other content Partner adds to the Portal, to run and promote the network. Partner confirms it has the rights to that content and that it is accurate.

## 7. Confidentiality and Privacy

Each party will protect the other's non-public information and the personal information of Agents and clients it receives, and will use it only for the transaction it relates to. Orbyt's [Privacy Policy](https://portal.orbytconnect.com/privacy.html) explains how Orbyt handles personal information.

## 10. Ending

Either party may end these Terms at any time with written notice, including by email. Orbyt may pause or remove Partner immediately if a license lapses, if Partner breaches Section 2 or 4, or if Orbyt receives complaints it reasonably believes are serious. ## 11. Disputes

These Terms are governed by California law. Before starting any proceeding, the party with a dispute will send the other a written notice and the parties will try in good faith to resolve it for thirty (30) days. Neither party will bring a claim as part of a class or representative action. Either party may bring a qualifying claim in small claims court instead.
